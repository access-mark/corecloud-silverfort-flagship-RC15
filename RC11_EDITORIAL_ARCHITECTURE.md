# RC11 — CoreCloud editorial architecture

## Purpose
Outcome first. Explain the business reality in plain language. Show the CoreCloud approach. Invite the next useful question.

## Voice
Senior-level thinking, straightforward wording, no empty marketing claims. Explain technical terms when needed. No promise of guaranteed security or delivery.

## Reader paths
Homepage → Workload Venue → Identity Security → Contact; Homepage → Platform → Orchestrator → How It Works → Assessments; specialist entry pages stand alone and link to a related next question.

## Editorial controls
The `The point` statement is an outcome anchor; the next-question section creates the chapter bridge. Repeated generic decision-framework strips were removed from four pages where detailed frameworks already followed.

## Scope
34 page introductions and handoffs rewritten, preserving the remainder of each page, forms, scripts, and vendor descriptions. This is a broad first editorial pass, not a claim that every paragraph is final.

## Production gates
Rendered desktop/mobile review, factual and partner claims review, accessibility testing, actual FormSubmit submissions and receipt verification, external links and Lighthouse. Do not ship without these.
